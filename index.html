Why I Built This

I am an immigration specialist by trade, and for 15 years I helped families cross borders to find new lives. But long before that, my own family crossed rivers and mountains—not for papers, but for survival.

I built this museum not just to list names and dates, but to honor the silence between them. To ensure that the struggle of the Bourvari villages, the resilience of the 5 Brothers, and the vision of Markar and Morvarid are never lost to time. This is for the generations who will come after us—so they may know whose shoulders they stand upon.

- The Curator
